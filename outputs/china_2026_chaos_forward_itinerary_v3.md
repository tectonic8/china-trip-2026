### Hotel candidates — four bases, with a $350 ceiling

**Hard limit: USD $350 per room per night, including mandatory taxes and fees.** Apply it to every night, not just the stay average. This replaces the earlier unlimited hotel budget. A suite or villa sold as one unit must also fit $350 total per night; do not divide an expensive villa's price by its bedrooms to make it appear eligible.

Default room plan: one king room plus one twin room for three separate beds, with both rooms individually within the cap. That means a maximum of **$700 per night for the group**, not a target. Three private rooms would allow a maximum of $1,050; only use that arrangement if the group wants the extra privacy. Seek a good room comfortably below the ceiling before considering upgrades.

**Price verification:** these are revised candidates to price, not confirmed October offers. September 6 research verified property listings and found lower-price signals, but final room categories, occupancy, taxes and October availability remain unverified. For example, Indigo Shanghai's comparison-site price trend lists roughly $168–218 across the former October 21–24 stay, but that is a historical lowest-price indicator, not a bookable tax-inclusive quote or guaranteed river view. [Price-trend evidence](https://www.trivago.com/en-US/oar/hotel-indigo-shanghai-on-the-bund-by-ihg?search=100-2964468)

The previous ultra-luxury hotels and villa upgrades are removed from the lodging plan. You can still visit a rooftop or book a cultural experience separately. Chengdu is included October 13–15; Hangzhou and Suzhou are day trips, not overnight stays. Hotel names elsewhere in the agenda are provisional first choices, subject to this price rule.
