### Hotel picks — three per city, with a $350 ceiling

**Hard limit: USD $350 per room per night, including mandatory taxes and fees.** Apply it to every night, not just the stay average. This replaces the earlier unlimited hotel budget. A suite or villa sold as one unit must also fit $350 total per night; do not divide an expensive villa's price by its bedrooms to make it appear eligible.

Default room plan: one king room plus one twin room for three separate beds, with both rooms individually within the cap. That means a maximum of **$700 per night for the group**, not a target. Three private rooms would allow a maximum of $1,050; only use that arrangement if the group wants the extra privacy. Seek a good room comfortably below the ceiling before considering upgrades.

**Price verification:** these are revised candidates to price, not confirmed October offers. September 6 research verified property listings and found lower-price signals, but final room categories, occupancy, taxes and October availability remain unverified. For example, Indigo Shanghai's comparison-site price trend lists roughly $168–218 across the former October 21–24 stay, but that is a historical lowest-price indicator, not a bookable tax-inclusive quote or guaranteed river view. [Price-trend evidence](https://www.trivago.com/en-US/oar/hotel-indigo-shanghai-on-the-bund-by-ihg?search=100-2964468)

The previous ultra-luxury hotels and villa upgrades are removed from the lodging plan. You can still visit a rooftop or book a cultural experience separately. Chengdu is now included October 14–16; Suzhou is an overnight October 21–22. Hotel names elsewhere in the agenda are provisional first choices, subject to this price rule.

#### Chongqing

1. **[Indition High Altitude Hotel](https://www.trip.com/hotels/chongqing-hotel-detail-88308956/chongqing-indition-high-altitude-hotel/) — first choice for cyberpunk atmosphere.** Price ordinary river-view rooms first; the listing includes Peninsula and Crystal Corridor categories. Keep the 120 m² two-bedroom river-view duplex with king plus twins only if the entire unit fits $350. Drop the automatic crystal-pool upgrade. Best for vertical-city views and Chaotianmen access; service is more boutique than grand-hotel.
2. **[Atour S Chongqing Jiefangbei Hongyadong Riverview](https://www.trip.com/hotels/chongqing-hotel-detail-71678942/atour-s-hotel-chongqing-jiefangbei-hongyadong-riverview/) — practical alternative with river-view potential.** Request one king and one twin, checking the view category explicitly. 3. **[InterContinental Chongqing Raffles City](https://www.ihg.com/intercontinental/hotels/gb/en/chongqing/chghb/hoteldetail/rooms) — conditional landmark upgrade.** Check entry-level king/twin rooms against the cap; no automatic club, suite or premium river-view booking. Keep only if the final quote fits. The Raffles architecture is the attraction, and an ordinary room may be enough.

#### Hangzhou

1. **[West Lake State Guesthouse](https://www.trip.com/hotels/hangzhou-hotel-detail-445394/west-lake-state-guesthouse/) — first choice for Chinese garden character.** Western-lake grounds and the property's state-guesthouse history make it a strong replacement for the earlier ultra-luxury resort. 3. **[Midtown Shangri-La, Hangzhou](https://www.shangri-la.com/hangzhou/midtownshangrila/) — best for a short, busy city stay.** Price standard king/twin rooms; lake view only if it still fits. Convenient for lakefront evenings, shopping and transport. Less unusual than the garden properties, but potentially the most efficient base.

**Hotel-independent experiences:** book the tea-country guide, guqin teacher and craft session directly or through the chosen hotel. These sessions must be arranged with the new provider rather than assumed to transfer from the previous resort. The former resort village walk becomes a walk through the chosen hotel's grounds; Lingyin now needs a drive.

#### Shanghai — one hotel for the entire stay

1. **[Hotel Indigo Shanghai on the Bund](https://www.hotelindigo.com/hotels/gb/en/shanghai/shgnb/hoteldetail) — first choice for local design plus riverfront atmosphere.** Price ordinary king/twin rooms, then ask what a river view adds. South Bund location works well for Yu Garden and tailoring; allow travel to the northern Bund and nightlife.
2. **[The Yangtze Boutique Shanghai](https://www.trip.com/hotels/shanghai-hotel-detail-426315/the-yangtze-boutique-shanghai/) — best for historic Art Deco character and central convenience.** Price standard king/twin rooms. 3. **[Broadway Mansions Hotel / Shanghai Dasha](https://www.trip.com/hotels/shanghai-hotel-detail-671/broadway-mansions-hotel) — best for a historic landmark by Suzhou Creek and the Bund.** Price a standard room, then a specifically river-facing category if affordable. **My call:** keep one Shanghai base for October 22–25 instead of the previous two-hotel luxury split. Use the recovered hotel-change block for sightseeing. Shanghai Tower's observation deck and your rooftop nights remain separate activities.

1. **[Niccolo Chengdu](https://www.niccolohotels.com/en/niccolo-chengdu) — first candidate for your upscale urban preference.** Price the entry-level king/twin categories within IFS; temple views and suites are extras, not requirements. Keep only if every night's complete rate meets the ceiling.

More residential than theatrical, with useful central-city access.

For all six cities: compare the complete quote for the chosen dates and bed arrangement. Reject any night over $350 after mandatory charges; changing to a cheaper room category is preferable to quietly relaxing the cap. No reservations have been made.

## Weather and failure logic

Zhangjiajie is now tighter, not weather-proof:

- If October 16 canyon is canceled, do Golden Whip then. October 19 may then support a short bridge-only visit if confirmed opening, road time and station buffer fit; do not promise the full canyon/bungee package.
- If a whole mountain day is lost, one of the core sights may be lost. Recovering a full day requires an explicit decision to shorten Hangzhou/Suzhou; no hidden rescue day exists.

- Outside Zhangjiajie, choose indoor cultural time for rain rather than forcing unsafe walks. The lake show, outdoor viewpoints and rooftop plans remain weather-dependent.
