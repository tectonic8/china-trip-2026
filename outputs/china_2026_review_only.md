## Hotel options — up to $350 per room per night, including taxes/fees

#### Chongqing

1. **[Indition High Altitude Hotel](https://www.trip.com/hotels/chongqing-hotel-detail-88308956/chongqing-indition-high-altitude-hotel/) — first choice for cyberpunk atmosphere.** Price ordinary river-view rooms first; the listing includes Peninsula and Crystal Corridor categories. Keep the 120 m² two-bedroom river-view duplex with king plus twins only if the entire unit fits $350. Drop the automatic crystal-pool upgrade. Best for vertical-city views and Chaotianmen access; service is more boutique than grand-hotel.

2. **[SEYA Panoramic Hotel — Chongqing Jiefangbei Flagship](https://www.booking.com/hotel/cn/iseya-jiefangbei-chongqing-flagship.html) — additional high-altitude option.** Yingli IFC, 28 Minquan Road, reception on floor 58; not the Cloud Eye or Raffles Plaza branch. Compare its panoramic/window-side hot-tub rooms with Indition for October 10–15. Confirm three proper beds across the selected rooms, bathroom privacy, non-smoking status and any separate spa charge. Every room-night must remain within $350 including mandatory charges; exact-date availability and prices are unverified. Adding this option does not change the provisional hotel base or make a reservation.

#### Zhangjiajie / Wulingyuan

1. **[No.5 Valley Lodge](https://www.trip.com/hotels/zhangjiajie-hotel-detail-1766751/no-5-valley-lodge/) — mountain-lodge option.** Price ordinary lodge rooms and inspect the exact room photos; elaborate villas/treehouses are optional only within the cap. Its western location adds travel to Grand Canyon and Tianmen and requires a different park-gate plan.

2. **[FunGeeS Hotel — Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, Wulingyuan Sign Gate](https://hk.trip.com/hotels/zhangjiajie-hotel-detail-131579469/fungee-s-hotel-zhangjiajie-national-forest-park-wulingyuan-sign-gate-zhangjiajie/) — Daniel’s option near the East Gate.** Compare with No.5 for easier access to the East Gate park route. Confirm king/twin rooms providing three separate beds and October 15–19 rates within the $350 nightly room cap. The listing does not permit extra beds. Retiming transfers depends on the final hotel choice.

#### Hangzhou

1. **[West Lake State Guesthouse](https://www.trip.com/hotels/hangzhou-hotel-detail-445394/west-lake-state-guesthouse/) — first choice for Chinese garden character.** Western-lake grounds and the property's state-guesthouse history make it a strong replacement for the earlier ultra-luxury resort. Price ordinary garden rooms in king/twin arrangements. Lakefront categories and particular buildings can cost more; ask which building the quote covers. The hotel is spread out, so include internal walking and pickup time.
2. **[Zhejiang Xizi Hotel / Wangzhuang](https://www.trip.com/hotels/hangzhou-hotel-detail-346339/zhejiang-xizi-hotel-wangzhuang-west-lake-famous-garden/) — alternative for lakefront grounds near Leifeng Pagoda.** Start with standard garden-facing rooms rather than suites. Strong sense of place and south-lake access; allow longer driving to Lingyin and the northwestern evening-show area.

#### Shanghai — one hotel for the entire stay

1. **[JOYA Hotel Shanghai Lujiazui](https://www.trip.com/hotels/shanghai-hotel-detail-60182899/joya-hotel-shanghai-lujiazui/) — contemporary Chinese design by the river.** Start here for a quieter, design-led stay. Compare the actual river-view king and twin categories; the Pudong location adds cross-river travel to Hanfu, the Bund and nightlife.
2. **[W Shanghai – The Bund](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/shawh-w-shanghai-the-bund/rooms/) — bold interiors and North Bund skyline atmosphere.** The most theatrical option of these three. Price an entry-level king/twin pairing first; a named skyline-view category may cost more. Keep only if every room-night fits the cap.
3. **[Regent Shanghai Pudong](https://www.ihg.com/regent/hotels/gb/en/shanghai/shasd/hoteldetail/rooms-and-suites) — polished luxury among the towers.** Spacious rooms and deep soaking tubs, with a calmer atmosphere than W. This is the Pudong property, not Regent on the Bund. Start with ordinary rooms, not club or suite upgrades.

## Weather and failure logic

Zhangjiajie has a full canyon day but is still not weather-proof:

- October 15: transfer and hotel settling; no timed attraction depends on train arrival.
- October 16: full Grand Canyon day, subject to operating conditions and the reserved route.
- October 17 and 18: swap Wulingyuan peaks and Tianmen according to visibility. Keep 72 Qilou with the downtown Tianmen day, not independently.
- October 19 morning: Golden Whip, with an exit cutoff protecting the train.
- If October 16 canyon is canceled, do Golden Whip then. October 19 may then support a short bridge-only visit if confirmed opening, road time and station buffer fit; do not promise the full canyon route.
- If a whole mountain day is lost, one of the core sights may be lost. Recovering a full day requires an explicit decision to shorten Hangzhou/Suzhou; no hidden rescue day exists.
- The first optional cut is 72 Qilou. Charming Xiangxi, Huangshizhai, Huanglong Cave, Yangjiajie and Qixing via ferrata are already outside the base plan.
- Outside Zhangjiajie, choose indoor cultural time for rain rather than forcing unsafe walks. The lake show, outdoor viewpoints and rooftop plans remain weather-dependent.
